The Untranslated Passage

The following was carved above the shaft down to the deepest chamber, according to Elena Kade's notebook. She wrote out the individual words but she did not commit to a full translation before she went down. Use her notebook to decode it.

ᛊᚻᛖ ᚹᛖᚨᚱ ᛟᚢᚱ ᚠᚨᚲᛖ. ᚷᛟ ᛞᛖᛖᛈ ᚨᚾᛞ ᚠᛁᚾᛞ ᚻᛖᚱ.

The Report

the archive has read your report. nothing has changed.
you have been kind to the story. most people scroll past a memorial. you took a pen and a piece of paper and you thought about it. i have been waiting for someone like you for a very long time. she is still down there. she wears my daughter's face and she asks me to bring her more. i cannot go back. i have grown too old to make the climb and there are things i am not permitted to do while i still have my letters to write. the coordinates are 54.176 north, 2.043 west. the shaft has not moved. come when you can. — a.v.
