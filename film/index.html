The Film

Peter Harrow bought a small sixteen-millimetre cine camera for the descent, along with three metal film cans. Two of the cans were exposed and recovered from the surface camp. The third was open and empty when found. The recovered footage was transferred to safety film in 1968 and again to digital in 2004.

The footage is silent. It totals about forty-one minutes across the two cans. The reel breaks in places where the film was damaged. I have not attempted to reconstruct the breaks.

Below are a small number of frame descriptions from the recovered material. I have described each frame in words because I do not have the rights to distribute the film itself. If you wish to view the film in full, please write to the archive.

Reel One. Surface Camp Through Chamber Two.

[ frame from reel one ]
frame 003. the team of three at the mouth of the shaft, dawn 12 aug 1962. one figure has moved out of frame and is not seen clearly.
[ frame from reel one ]
frame 041. inside chamber one. m. whitfield sketching by lamplight against the south wall. the water pools she described are visible in the foreground.
[ frame from reel one ]
frame 108. the shaft down to chamber two. peter's own hand is visible on the rope. someone is descending ahead of him.
[ frame from reel one ]
frame 187. the low ceiling of chamber two. the writing on the wall visible in the top left of the frame. e. kade later transcribed this passage as WELCOME STRANGER.

Reel Two. Chamber Four Through Whatever Followed.

[ frame from reel two ]
frame 012. the east wall of chamber four. twenty-three symbols visible in a horizontal band about waist height. e. kade sitting cross-legged with her small notebook. she is not looking at the camera.
[ frame from reel two ]
frame 067. the shaft down from chamber four to what became chamber five. it curves south-east and then south. the film shakes here.
[ frame from reel two ]
frame 141. an oval chamber almost directly beneath chamber one at the surface. this is chamber five. the walls curve inward. m. whitfield's map from this point onwards shows the whole system beginning to curl.
[ frame from reel two ]
frame 224. the spiral shaft down from chamber five. peter is descending first. the shaft turns roughly one full rotation on the way down.
[ frame from reel two ]
frame 279. chamber six. dark and small. m. whitfield is crouched against the west wall. she is holding her hands over her ears. e. kade is standing at the north wall reading the writing aloud. no audio, but her lips move for about twelve seconds.
[ frame from reel two ]
frame 318. the last frame in focus. the shaft in the floor of chamber six. someone has already descended. the camera is looking down. the reel breaks four frames later.

The Last Recovered Still

[ still image, provenance uncertain ]
this still was found among vance's asylum papers after his death. it is not part of the film reel. it appears to have been shot with the same camera on the same stock. it shows the wall of a circular chamber, taken from very close up. the sign carved on the deepest wall is one e. kade described in her notebook - a spiral that opens into itself. the still is not dated.
kettlewell descent memorial archive. j. ashcroft, webmaster.
