The Team

Three cavers descended into the Kettlewell shaft on the morning of 12 August 1962. One returned. This page holds a small biography of each of them, drawn from the materials their families were kind enough to share with this archive.

  • Dr. Alistair Vance - lead. geologist. the one who came back.
  • Margaret Whitfield - cartographer. surface mapping. Vance's fiancée.
  • Peter Harrow - cameraman. documentary filmmaker.

The team was funded by a small grant from the Sheffield University geological society, which limited the roster to three named participants. All three were amateur cavers with several prior descents to their credit.
