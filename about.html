About This Archive

My name is John Ashcroft. I have maintained this archive for over fifty years. I am not a professional historian and I have no formal training in caving or in the study of language. I am a private man who has spent a very long time reading about the events of August 1962 and trying to make small sense of them.

I was ten years old when the story broke. I lived in a village not far from Kettlewell. On the 16th day of August I saw a small procession of vehicles come down from the ridge. Three descended, one returned. My mother told me later that a great deal of grayish smoke had been seen on the hill in the days before, though nobody could explain it. I do not know what to make of this, even now.

I have been in written correspondence with the surviving members of the families for many years. I have collected what they were willing to send me. Dr. Vance's own journal pages were donated by his sister in 1974. Peter Harrow's father sent me the film reel in 1968, along with a small envelope of photographs. M. Whitfield's mother sent me a small parcel of her daughter's letters, which I have not published out of respect. Elena Kade had no family that I could find.

I make no claim to know what happened underground. I only know what the team wrote and photographed. I have not visited the site. I do not intend to.

How To Use This Archive

Read carefully. Nothing here is difficult to find. Nothing here is a puzzle. Everything is where I placed it, in plain view, and if you read it slowly and think about what you are reading, you will come to your own conclusions about what happened on the ridge.

I have made a small effort to preserve the writers' original spelling and punctuation. I have added footnotes only where a modern reader might not know a term.

If you find an inconsistency between two pages, that is because the original documents themselves are inconsistent. I have not corrected the record. I do not believe it is my place.
